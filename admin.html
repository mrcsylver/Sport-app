One exercise, one amount, and an optional time window. That is deliberately narrower than the built-in quests can be: it is the shape that cannot produce something nobody is able to finish.

Deleting a player removes their account and every workout they logged, in every league. Deleting a league removes it for everyone, but its members keep their logs in any other league they are in. Neither can be undone.
