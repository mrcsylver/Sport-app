Terms of use

Iron League · last updated 11 October 2026

The short version. It is a free leaderboard for calisthenics, run by one person as a side project. Log honestly, be decent to the people in your league, and understand that nothing here is a coach, a doctor, or a guarantee that the server will be up.

What this is

Iron League is a free app for scoring and ranking bodyweight training between people who know each other. There is nothing to buy. If that ever changes, these terms change first and you get told.

It runs on the honour system

Nothing verifies that you did the reps. The app cannot tell the difference between fifty real push-ups and a number you typed, and it is not going to try. Inflating your numbers does not beat the app — it just makes the leaderboard meaningless for everybody in your league, including you.

League owners can remove somebody who is obviously making it up. That is the whole enforcement mechanism and it is deliberately human.

Your account

  • You are responsible for keeping your restore code. It is the key to your account. If you lose it and have no email or Google sign-in attached, the account cannot be recovered — there is no way to prove it was yours.
  • Pick a name your league would be happy to see. Impersonating somebody else, or using a name that harasses them, gets the account removed.
  • One person, one account. Making extra accounts to inflate a league is the one thing that will get you removed without a conversation.

This is not fitness advice

Iron League counts what you tell it and turns it into points. It does not know your injuries, your medical history or your limits, and the quests and suggestions it shows are generated from a table — not written for you.

Training is at your own risk. If you are unsure whether an exercise is safe for you, ask someone qualified rather than a leaderboard. Chasing points into an injury is a real way to get hurt and the app cannot stop you.

What is promised about uptime

Nothing. It runs on free hosting. It may be slow, it may be down, and a bug may lose data. Backups are taken nightly, but this is a side project and not a service with a support contract behind it.

What is promised: if something breaks, you will be told honestly what happened rather than quietly.

Ending it

Leave a league whenever you like, from the app. Want the whole account gone? Email mrcsylver@gmail.com and it is deleted, along with every workout on it.

Accounts can be removed for cheating at scale, harassing other players, or trying to break the app for everyone else. Nothing else.

The boring part

The app is provided as-is, with no warranty. To the extent the law allows, the person running it is not liable for lost data, lost points, injury, or anything that follows from using it. Where the law does not allow that, the law wins.
