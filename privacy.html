Privacy

Iron League · last updated 11 October 2026

The short version. Iron League stores the name you pick, the workouts you log, and — only if you choose to sign in — your email address. There are no adverts, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third party is sent your data to sell. Nothing is shared with anyone outside the leagues you join.

Who is responsible

Iron League is run by one person, not a company. If you want your data, or want it gone, email mrcsylver@gmail.com and you will get a reply from the person who can actually do it.

What is stored, and why

WhatWhyOptional?
Display nameSo the leaderboard has something to show. It does not have to be your real name, and most people's isn't. Required
Workouts — exercise, amount, date The entire point of the app. This is what is scored and ranked. Required
Email address Only so you can sign back in on another phone. It is never used to send you anything — the app has no mailing list and no way to email you. Optional
Bodyweight Only to score gym lifts, where points depend on load relative to bodyweight. It is never shown to anyone else, ever. Optional
Look — emblem, banner, colours, figure Cosmetic choices you make.Optional
Restore code An 8-character code that moves your profile to a new phone. Generated for you
About bodyweight. Under EU law this counts as health data, which gets stricter treatment. So: it is optional, you can leave it blank and use everything except gym-lift scoring, it is never displayed to other players, and clearing the field deletes it.

What is NOT collected

  • No location, ever.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health-app data.
  • No advertising identifiers, no cookies for tracking, no analytics service. The app does not know which page you came from.
  • No payment details — nothing in the app charges money.

Who can see what

People in a league with you see your name, your emblem, your points and the workouts you logged in that league. That is the whole idea of a leaderboard.

Nobody sees your email, your bodyweight or your restore code. Not other players, not league owners. The database enforces this with row-level security rather than trusting the app to hide it.

Where it lives

The database is hosted by Supabase on servers in Oregon, United States. Using the app means your data is transferred outside the EU to there. The app itself is served as static files by GitHub Pages.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells the app your email address and nothing else, and the sign-in happens on Google's pages, not ours — your password is never typed into Iron League.

How long

Your workouts stay until you delete your account. There is no automatic expiry: a league's history is the thing people come back for.

An account may be parked if it has been unused for a long time and somebody else needs the space. Parking deletes nothing — it hides the account and frees a slot — and it comes back the moment there is room.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or the UK you have the right to see your data, correct it, take it elsewhere, or have it erased. In practice:

  • See or export it — email and ask. You will get a file.
  • Correct it — your name, email, bodyweight and look are all editable in the app under Leagues.
  • Delete it — email and ask, and the account and every workout on it are removed. This cannot be undone.
  • Complain — if you are in France you can complain to the CNIL.

The legal basis is your consent for the optional things, and performing the service you asked for — showing you a leaderboard — for the rest.

Children

Iron League is not aimed at children under 16 and should not be used by them without a parent's involvement.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the app will say so rather than hoping you re-read it.
